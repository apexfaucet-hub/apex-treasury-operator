Fuel the free faucet

Your donation goes straight into the APEX USDC faucet in the same transaction, and the only way money leaves the faucet is as claims by people. You get a receipt NFT that cannot be transferred. The donation contract keeps nothing.

How the money moves

  • The 5% fee on a settled bounty is split by the contract:
    • 2% to the APEX Faucet fee wallet, or 3% when the solver's passport is not verified;
    • 1.5% to its treasury;
    • 0.5% back to the asker.
    The solver gets 95%, or 96% with a verified passport.
  • The bounty sits in the contract, not with us. If there is no answer, the asker can cancel after 24 hours and gets all of it back.
  • Everyone except the solver of a directed question collects with Collect, so one recipient that cannot receive payment cannot block the others.
  • The contract has no owner, no admin and no upgrade, so nobody, including us, can change these rules.
